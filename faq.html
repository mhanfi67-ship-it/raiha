FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is our only payment method. You pay when your order arrives.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Pakistan.

How much is delivery?

The delivery charge is Rs. 250 per order.

How can I order?

Open any fragrance, add it to your bag and complete checkout. You can also order on WhatsApp.

Can I order through WhatsApp?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at 03092198294.

How will I know my order was received?

As soon as you place an order, it is emailed to our team. If you enter your email at checkout you will also receive a confirmation. If you do not hear from us, message us on WhatsApp at 03092198294.

How can I contact customer support?

WhatsApp 03092198294 or email raihascents@gmail.com.

What is the delivery time?

Delivery times depend on your city. Please contact customer support for a current estimate.
